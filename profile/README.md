## Patent Notice

The ZEAM system and methods are the subject of pending U.S. patent applications owned by ZEAM
Foundation, LLC — **26 in total: 21 provisional and 5 non-provisional utility applications.** Every
application number, filing date and full text is published in the
[IP ledger](https://github.com/zeam-foundation/zeam-ip).

Provisional applications are never published by the USPTO, and the non-provisionals filed
17 September 2025 publish at eighteen months from their earliest priority date. Absence from public
patent search before then is expected and is not evidence in either direction — the ledger is the
record.

## Overview

ZEAM is a memory-first substrate architecture. The patent portfolio covers:

- Distributed memory-mesh coordination
- Deterministic (non-generative) AI execution
- Genesis-anchored governance
- Layered identity and privacy systems
- Memory-pressure-based coordination
- Non-token economic models

## What is running

**[ZEAM :: Prism](https://www.zeamprism.com)** — pay-per-millisecond Ethereum and Base RPC over
Model Context Protocol, paid over x402 and settled on Base. No signup and no API key: the buyer is an
agent with a wallet. Endpoint `https://mcp.zeamprism.com/mcp`, docs at
[llms.txt](https://mcp.zeamprism.com/llms.txt), and the free `sample` tool needs no wallet at all.

**[x402-mcp-bridge](https://github.com/zeam-labs/x402-mcp-bridge)** — MIT-licensed client that puts a
wallet in front of any x402-paid MCP server.
